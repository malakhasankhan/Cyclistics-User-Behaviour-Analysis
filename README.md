#  Cyclistic User Behavior Analysis  
*Unlocking growth through behavioral segmentation*

##  Project Overview

Cyclistic, a bike-share company operating in Chicago, seeks to convert casual riders into annual members.  
This project dives deep into **2024 ride data** to uncover **behavioral patterns** that distinguish casual and member users — with the goal of informing targeted marketing strategies to drive membership growth.

We explored when people ride, how long their trips last, where they go, and why those patterns matter.

---

##  Key Questions

- How do casual and annual members behave differently?
- Are there patterns in time, trip length, or routes?
- What clues in the data point to **why** casual riders aren't converting?
- How can Cyclistic design smarter interventions to change that?

---

##  Summary of Findings

- **Casual riders** take longer, more varied, and leisure-oriented trips.
- **Annual members** use the service more predictably — for functional commutes.
- Usage patterns differ **by day**, **hour**, and **trip direction**.
- The data reveals **clear opportunities** to reposition Cyclistic's offerings for casual riders.

- **SQL (SQLite + DBeaver)** — Data cleaning, merging, feature engineering
- **R (tidyverse)** — Visualization and exploratory analysis
- **Tableau** — Interactive dashboard prototyping
- **Google Docs** — Final storytelling and report writing

---

##  Recommendations

- Launch **summer bundles** and **tourist day passes**
- Partner with **travel websites and hotel booking platforms**
- Highlight **cost-saving** benefits of annual plans
- Deploy marketing around **top casual-use stations** during peak hours
